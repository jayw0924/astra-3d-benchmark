## Visual review (author assessment, not user preference)

Every panel of all four finish contact sheets and all36 native center crops was loaded with the vision tool and inspected. Native original/environment glass and native original/environment/repaired-grounding Carmine exteriors were additionally inspected. Desktop/mobile gallery screenshots inspected; all72 images decode with alt text and no horizontal overflow at1280px and390px.

| Dimension | Observation |
|---|---|
| Body readability | Environment treatment lifts strongly crushed Carmine/Graphite door and rear-body areas, exposing broad curvature and lower trim. Estuary changes less dramatically but has smoother broad highlights. |
| Highlight character | Broad studio reflections replace the baseline's largely dark fields with gradients. Carmine still has very bright shoulder speckle; Pearly has broad nearly-white highlights and looks paler/flatter. This is an observed tradeoff, not a numeric realism gain. |
| Glass | Broad diagonal white windshield reflection is more distracting and masks more dash/interior detail than original. Existing bright point glint persists. Original is preferable for seeing interior detail in these views. No material/exposure correction was used to hide that negative result. |
| Wheels | Tire shoulder, rim edges and red brake area become easier to distinguish, but deep glossy black spokes remain dark and thin edges alias at600×400. Existing wheel/rim treatment is unchanged. |
| Grounding | Repaired filtered floor shadows remove the ungrounded grid presentation and give contact context; side/rear views retain a directional cast shape, not a physically accurate area-light penumbra. The wheel close-up can still look slightly lifted: floor uses whole-model minimum, while individual source wheel bounds differ. No geometry was moved to hide this. |
| Finish identity | Red Carmine, pale iridescent Pearly, charcoal/iridescent Graphite and teal Estuary remain distinct in all stages. Source metallic noise/iridescence and seam-edge aliasing remain visible. No material-quality claim follows. |

No final blank, wrong-finish, camera-in-model, missing-target or browser-error frame found. Detail views intentionally crop the car around their named surface; exterior framing is unchanged and relatively distant, matching the live control camera rather than refitting for a more flattering candidate.

## Attempts and bounded repair

1. Initial long capture ended with exit137 after26 checkpointed images (24 original +2 environment). Cause was not conclusively isolated; do not claim measured OOM or timeout. PNGs were rehashed before recovery; originals were not recaptured merely because the process ended.
2. Six-case browser batches completed the remaining environment views. During grounded native review, a harsh polygonal default-radius directional shadow was rejected. The first grounding batch and part of the next had produced11 frames when the descendant capture processes were stopped. Both the early and last manifests, source and all11 rejected PNGs are retained under `attempts/grounded-v1/`. Wrapper cancellation alone did not stop its descendants; explicit descendant termination was needed before recovery.
3. Test-first bounded correction: supported PCFShadowMap (Three r186 removed PCFSoftShadowMap), radius6, opacity0.18. No illumination, material, camera or exposure edits. `shadow-repair.json` preserves old/new pins and the48 retained unaffected IDs; `repair-parity.json` proves exact PNG hashes for one original and one environment exterior after the source correction. These two parity probes are not a whole-matrix rerender-determinism claim.
4. Final set comprises48 retained original/environment images and24 freshly corrected grounding images. There are11 separate rejected image files and2 parity render probes without new gallery images. Ten completed raw sessions include historical environment captures, one rejected-grounding session, the parity session and four corrected-grounding batches; do not call them ten independent final experiments. Incomplete/interrupted contexts retain logs/checkpoints and are excluded from complete-session summaries.
