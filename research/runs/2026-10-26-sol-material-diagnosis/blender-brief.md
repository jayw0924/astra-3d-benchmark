## What Blender would add

- Interactive face-orientation and split/custom-normal overlays, topology/wireframe inspection at named regions, smooth/flat discontinuity inspection and edit-mode correspondence checks. These can help localize an interpolation problem that unit normal-length checks cannot diagnose.
- UV-editor inspection of islands, overlap, stretch, seam placement and per-surface texture density; shader-node/normal-map inspection at authored repeat scales rather than merely reading UV coordinate ranges.
- Scene-unit and dimension review, object scale/negative transforms, actual windshield shell thickness/backfaces and overlapping surfaces. The absence of a glTF volume extension is not a measurement of geometric shell thickness.
- Editable, separately saved scene and a reference-render workflow for art direction. It does not automatically reproduce Three.js PMREM, screen-space transmission, ACES implementation, shadow sampling, texture transcode/filtering or browser rasterization.
