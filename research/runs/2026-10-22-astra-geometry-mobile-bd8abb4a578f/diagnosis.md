# Initial Mobile LOD hypothesis, frozen before authoring

Named deployment metric: uncompressed GLB delivery bytes relative to immutable web-product-estuary. Secondary structural counts: triangles and distinct POSITION accessor entries. No mobile FPS or GPU speed inference.

Ranked plausible costs: (1) triangle geometry/attribute payload (213195 triangles, 162646 distinct POSITION entries); (2) 107 primitive submissions, unchanged here; (3) embedded textures, kept byte-exact; (4) 30 materials including transmission/clearcoat/iridescence and shader complexity, unchanged; (5) compilation, not isolated by file reduction; (6) continuous rendering, an application behavior outside this package. This ranking prioritizes the experiment's addressable delivery cost, not measured runtime bottlenecks. Source-inventory.json records per-mesh counts and actual texture bytes.

Source inspection identifies large repeated rims (meshes 88/90/92/94, 12176 triangles each), wipers (3, 24336), rear painted panels (31,12984) and lower panels (39,13360). Prior fixed-view evidence explicitly implicated hood/glass and BodyPillars interpolation; retain the nine final Safe exclusions byte-exact. Do NOT simply assume that set is adequate for more aggressive Mobile reduction: additionally protect the entire wipers, rear/side glass and gaskets, lamp surfaces, mirrors, and tire meshes (87/89/91/93). Wipers are large but their thin structural forms are more valuable than a quota. Wheels retain original tire circularity and transforms; rims get a separate cautious ratio 0.65 / error 0.00005 / locked borders rather than the general ratio 0.50 / error 0.0001. Primary interior and body surfaces use the general local simplification with untouched attribute tuples and locked borders. This may deliver much less than 45–65% reduction; that is an honest outcome, not grounds for a parameter search.

Exact protected mesh set: 1,2,3,4,30,33,34,35,36,37,38,40,41,42,43,45,50,51,52,53,54,69,70,71,72,73,87,89,91,93. All primitives in those meshes are preserved. General and rim simplification runs after per-primitive compact/detach; no global weld, merges, texture changes, normals regeneration or hierarchy changes. Derived from the disclosed final Safe recipe, not conceptually blind. Both binaries reconstruct independently from original bytes, never from Safe or another candidate.

Intended deviation: fewer internal triangles and interpolated shading differences on eligible surfaces; these are NOT accepted visual differences. Risk: reflective panel interpolation, rim spokes and primary interior silhouettes could still regress. Stop on semantic failures; do not correct or optimize again without feedback. One initial recipe, zero planned correction rounds. Rollback: unnecessary, all outputs isolated and public Safe remains unchanged. Verification: independent raw/NodeIO checks, official pinned validator, independent byte-identical rebuild, frozen native control/candidate renders and exact replay of accepted controls. Headless/image statistics cannot establish aesthetic acceptance or hidden geometry quality; independent Sol visual review remains required. No DCC retopology is claimed.
