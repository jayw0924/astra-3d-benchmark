## Model/rig lifecycle

The existing loader hides License Plate, retains supplied materials, measures initial bounds, subtracts their center, lifts by half the original height, and rotates the root by −0.22 radians about Y. That centering/pose logic is unchanged. The new rig is created only after adding the transformed root and updating its world matrix, using a fresh world-space Box3. Observed bounds are min `[-1.7371991037440258,0,-2.375912426720423]`, max `[1.5197008011738995,1.3079541074180576,2.3618050295395916]`; floor Y is −0.003. Camera is the unchanged `[6.4,3.25,7.2]`, target `[0,.55,.1]`, FOV 32°, ACES exposure 1.05, background #ddd9d0.

A temporary original rig exists while loading. It is disposed before replacement by the shared `grounded-studio-refined` factory. No recipe constants or room refinement changed. Disposal restores environment, castShadow flags, shadow enabled/type/autoUpdate/needsUpdate, and releases floor/grid/shadow/environment resources. Viewer teardown is idempotent. Late model completion is disposed without creating another rig. Each asynchronously resolved material is registered immediately, including late completion after a sibling rejected; a viewer-lifetime disposed-resource set prevents shared material/texture double disposal. Stale variant results do not attach or invalidate shadows. Actual changed material assignments refresh transparent-caster eligibility and invalidate cached shadows; redundant finish assignment does not.

Real pointer-orbit verification exposed pre-existing reset drift from OrbitControls damping. A new test using real OrbitControls reproduced it before the minimal fix: drain pending deltas with damping disabled, then overwrite the home camera/target, update, and restore damping. Final desktop pointer drag/wheel/reset and mobile button/reset checks pass.

**Lighting rollback:** change post-load `preset: 'grounded-studio-refined'` to `preset: 'original'` in `src/lib/threeViewer.ts`, then `npm run build:app`.

## Native images and limitations

`before-original-{desktop,mobile}-native.png` were captured from the actual app before integration at the original default pose. `after-refined-{chromium,mobile-chromium}-*-native.png` are native app framebuffer exports for all four finishes at that same pose; Carmine `*-orbit.png` adds a second camera angle. Desktop canvas is 850×778, mobile framebuffer 353×440; the pre-change mobile compositor clip is 352×440 because of fractional CSS sizing. Before images include the DOM ready badge; after framebuffer images do not. These are not pixel-parity pairs. `images.json` pins image sizes/hashes, including rejected attempts clearly separated by path. `index.html` provides a static evidence review, not a substitute interactive application.

Continuous CPU rendering repeatedly stalled compositor screenshots. Tests pace application animation submissions at one-second intervals from page load; they do not change renderer settings, camera, lighting or material values. Accepted after images wrap one future application animation callback and call canvas.toDataURL immediately after that callback's own render. No alternate render harness, scene or preserveDrawingBuffer setting. Blank CDP attempts used wrong document/viewport clipping and are retained in `attempts-cdp-viewport-clip/`, not accepted as viewer images. Screenshot timeouts, the unsuccessful paused-scheduling attempt, a terminated batch and exit-137 logs are retained. Exact kill causes were not established; do not call them proven OOMs. These tests establish no production GPU performance.

All eight native finish images, original desktop/mobile images and the desktop orbit image were visually inspected. Body contours and lower side panels are more legible than the dark original Carmine presentation; the grid is replaced by a light contact shadow. The shadow remains under the stationary model while the camera moves; model bounds/floor remain invariant, correctly world-anchored. This is not a test of animated/deforming model shadows (there is no such control). The shadow is broad and faint, with limited tire-contact contrast rather than strong ambient occlusion.

Carmine retains sparkle and some sharply bright/splotchy hood transitions. Pearly remains pale silver with broad rainbow reflections, and white seating/glass reflections dominate the cabin. Torched keeps dark charcoal with colored edge bands; Estuary reads teal but retains uneven highlight patches on the front/hood. The windshield/roof remain reflective, obscuring some interior detail; dark wheels and some thin bright seams remain. These observations are residual appearance concerns, not attribution to damaged geometry, proof of realism or subjective approval. No paint/glass/model edits were made to hide them. Only pre-existing Rim1/Rim2 treatment mutates material parameters. Material/geometry diagnosis is a later authorized phase.

The finish images precede the reset-only bug fix; the lighting/material/default camera at capture did not change. Final Carmine interaction tests were rerun after that fix without replacing the accepted images. No new `run.json`, final-run claim or preference record was invented. Parent review/provenance closeout remains separate.
